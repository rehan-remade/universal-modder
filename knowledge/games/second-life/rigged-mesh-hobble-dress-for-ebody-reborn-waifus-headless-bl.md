# Rigged mesh hobble dress for eBody Reborn + Waifus: headless Blender build, own Collada writer, .anim animations

> A wearable rigged-mesh leather hobble corset dress for the eBody Reborn body with the Waifu breast add-on,
> with a PBR leather material (seams, topstitching, zippers), four LODs, two cutouts, and a hobble walk and
> stand that an LSL script swaps in through SL's animation override. Everything is generated by headless
> Blender scripts from the body's dev kit; the user had no 3D experience. Verified by the user in world on the
> Aditi beta grid with their own shape, over about a dozen upload iterations.

## Setup
- Viewer: Firestorm 7.2.4.80712, Windows 11. Firestorm ships SL's skeleton definitions in
  `<viewer>\character\avatar_skeleton.xml` and the shape-slider definitions in `avatar_lad.xml`; both were
  used as the source of truth.
- Blender 4.5.10 LTS, run headless (`blender -b <kit.blend> --python script.py`). **No Avastar** installed.
  4.5 still has the Collada exporter (gone in Blender 5.0), but see Gotcha 1: we didn't use it.
- eBody Reborn Dev Kit 2.0: obtained through eBody's application form (no store needed). It includes Blender
  kits for 2.79 / 2.93 / 3.6.7 / 5.0.1 (Avastar), Maya/DAE kits, and skin/material kits. The 3.6.7 .blend
  opens in Blender 4.5. Its terms forbid redistributing kit files (keep them and any .blend that contains the
  kit body out of git and releases) and require products to need the base Reborn body.
- Test loop: Firestorm **Build → Local Mesh** (free, only you see it; wear a prim, select it, Apply) for
  fit; the **Aditi** beta grid (free uploads) for anything needing a real upload (materials, scripts,
  animations).

## Route and why
Content route only: rigged mesh `.dae` + glTF material + animations, uploaded through the viewer, plus one
LSL script. Second Life is online; nothing about the viewer or protocol is touched.
- Fit against the body's official dev kit, used under its license. Never fit against mesh exported from the
  viewer or its cache (copybot-style ripping of another creator's body or clothing): SL's terms and the body
  makers' licenses forbid it, and the dev kit exists for exactly this job.
- Considered: building on SL's default avatar. Rejected: clothing has to be fitted to the body's dev kit
  (its shape and weights), otherwise it won't follow that body's sliders.
- Considered: Avastar (the usual SL rigging add-on, paid). Not installed; a ~200-line Collada writer
  (Gotcha 1) and an `.anim` writer (Gotcha 10) did the job without it.
- Considered: Maitreya LaraX instead of eBody. Its kit application is selective ("if your store is
  selected"); eBody's kit came quickly without a store.

## How the game works (what we had to learn)
- **Frames.** The kit rig in Blender faces −Y with +X as the avatar's left. SL's skeleton faces +X with +Y
  left, Z up. Export rotates the mesh by Rz(+90°). Every SL joint has an identity rest orientation, so a
  joint's local axes at rest are the world axes.
- **Rigged-mesh skinning.** The viewer uses the DAE's inverse bind matrices with its own skeleton:
  world(joint) is a translation chain of `pos` for bones, and T(pos)·R(rot, XYZ degrees)·S(scale) for
  collision volumes (BUTT, LEFT_PEC, CHEST…); IBM = inverse(world). The volumes' scale (0.05 to 0.2) must be
  in the IBM. Verified by matching the eBody kit's own SL-ready DAE (BUTT IBM translation 0.6/0/−9.67).
- **Rest pose.** SL's skeleton rests in a **T-pose**; the eBody kit rig rests in an **A-pose**. Animations
  are local rotations relative to SL's rest frame. To preview them on the kit rig, convert per bone:
  D = rotation(SL rest bone direction → kit rest direction), then posed world = W_SL · D⁻¹ · kit rest.
- **Shape sliders = bone transforms**, defined in `avatar_lad.xml`. Example: Breast Size (id 105) drives
  Big_Chest (id 626) with w = (v−0.5)/0.5 above 0.5; Big_Chest scales LEFT/RIGHT_PEC by +0.0273·w (base 0.05)
  and moves them (0.038, ±0.024, −0.016)·w metres in SL axes. At slider 79: ×1.32, 2.2 cm forward, 1.4 cm
  out, 0.9 cm down. Applying these to the kit rig in Blender reproduced in-world problems exactly; the kit's
  default shape hid them (Gotcha 6).
- **Uploader rules.** Physics shapes may not contain sliver triangles (`MAV_FOUND_DEGENERATE_TRIANGLES`).
  Error details, including the triangle's index and vertices, are in
  `%APPDATA%\Firestorm_x64\logs\Firestorm.log`. All LOD files should share one joint list.
- **Animations.** The `.bvh` importer expects Y-up, +Z-forward, inches; joints use the classic names
  (hip, abdomen, lThigh…); a channel order Z X Y equals Blender Euler 'YXZ'. It compresses keyframes on
  import (Gotcha 10). Firestorm also uploads SL's native **`.anim`** (LLKeyframeMotion v1.0) with **no
  settings dialog and no preview**: priority, loop and ease come from the file, the asset name from the
  filename.
- **Animation override.** `llSetAnimationOverride("Walking"/"Standing", anim)` from an attachment needs
  PERMISSION_OVERRIDE_ANIMATIONS, which attachments get without a dialog. Whichever object set a state last
  wins, so an AO can take it back; re-applying on a timer works. Reset the overrides on detach.
- **PBR materials** upload as a materials-only glTF (Build → Upload → Material). A material can't be applied
  to a Local Mesh surrogate; that needs a real upload (Aditi). How SL numbers a mesh's faces, and recoloring PBR
  faces from a HUD with glTF overrides, are in the
  [ballet boots note](rigged-wedge-ballet-boots-for-ebody-reborn-feet-with-a-pbr-c.md).

## Build steps
1. Get the dev kit; pick the body variant object (here `RebornBody+Waifus`, parented to armature `Avatar`).
2. Build the garment procedurally (`build_dress.py`), all in rest pose:
   - per height level, take the 2D convex hull of a body slice (arm vertices excluded by weight), offset 4 mm;
   - front and back panels as separate grids, welded below the armholes and at the collar;
   - push-out against a BVH of the skin, plus a "reverse" pass that pushes faces any skin vertex pokes through;
   - recompute UVs from final positions;
   - transfer weights with a DATA_TRANSFER modifier (nearest face, interpolated), drop forearm and hand
     groups, cap at 4 influences, normalise.
3. LODs: rebuild at lower ring counts (72/48/32/24 around), don't decimate. Every level then gets the same
   fit and UVs.
4. Export with your own Collada writer (Gotcha 1); pass the HIGH joint list to every LOD; use a 12-triangle
   box as the physics shape.
5. Texture: generate base colour / normal (OpenGL +Y) / ORM in numpy in UV space, plus a materials-only
   `.gltf`.
6. Animations: write SL-frame local rotations per frame, then emit `.anim` directly (or `.bvh`).
7. LSL: override Walking/Standing on attach, re-apply every 5 s, reset on detach.

## Verification
- **In world (the real oracle):** the user wore every version on Aditi and sent screenshots, some
  annotated (e.g. one region circled red "moves with the arms", another yellow "never moves"). That
  annotation is what cracked the armhole bug.
- **Automated Blender checks, with the user's shape applied:**
  - leg skin outside the posed dress over every walk/stand frame (hands excluded — Gotcha 9);
  - arm vertices behind the dress surface, arms down;
  - non-arm skin poking through, with the shape applied;
  - sharp corners along armhole edges (turning angle > 50°);
  - "WIDE": dress vertices beyond the torso width.
- **File checks:** the DAE inverse bind matrices against the kit's SL-ready DAE; `.anim` files decoded back
  to check headers, key counts and pelvis offsets.
- **Not verified:** other eBody add-ons or the plain Reborn body (the cleavage cutout needs Waifu); shapes
  other than the user's and the kit default; main-grid upload (done on Aditi only); land impact and fee
  numbers.

## Gotchas
1. **Rigging broken: all-zero inverse bind matrices.** Blender 4.5's Collada exporter on the Avastar kit rig
   (493 constraints, 6 drivers) writes 0 for every bind pose, with or without `open_sim` and in REST pose.
   It also can't express collision-volume scale. **Fix:** write the DAE yourself; take joint matrices from
   the viewer's `avatar_skeleton.xml`, compute IBM = inverse(world), and rotate the mesh Rz(+90°).
2. **Upload fails with MAV_FOUND_DEGENERATE_TRIANGLES.** The physics shape defaulted to the lowest LOD, which
   had a sliver triangle near the hem (named in Firestorm.log). **Fix:** a box `.dae` as the physics shape;
   worn attachments never use physics.
3. **Seams wobble and zig-zag in the texture.** UVs were assigned at layout, before smoothing and push-out
   moved the vertices. **Fix:** compute UVs from the final positions (u = angle around the body centre line,
   v = height; lift u on faces that straddle the wrap).
4. **Pointed flaps at the collar corners in world.** The closed collar ring started at 1.575 m, where a body
   slice includes the tops of the shoulders. **Fix:** start the collar band where the neck stands clear
   (~1.60 m) and shape it from the neck slice only; trim halter slices to the cut width + 3 cm.
5. **Dress "torn" at the back of the armpits with arms down.** The dress had no shoulder/upper-arm weights
   (all arm groups dropped) while the skin beneath has them, and arm and torso skin overlap there anyway.
   **Fix:** keep shoulder and upper-arm weights; cut a racer back clear of where the arm rests; slope-limit
   the armhole edge angle ring to ring (no single-ring jumps) so it is one smooth U.
6. **Armhole notch and tip only in world.** The kit's default shape hid them. Angles were measured from each
   slice's centroid, and at bust height the bust drags it about 10 cm forward. The "side seam", and the
   armhole bottom with it, landed on the side of the breast, 78% weighted to PEC; Breast Size 79 then pulled
   the front and back edges apart. **Fix:** hold the torso centre (from below the bust) through the armhole
   region; fade PEC out around the armhole bottom; reproduce the user's sliders from `avatar_lad.xml` in
   every test.
7. **Large breast slider pushes skin through the bust.** Weight smoothing had diluted the dress's PEC weight
   to ~0.65 where the skin's is ~0.98. **Fix:** set the dress's PEC to at least 0.97 × the average of the 6
   nearest skin vertices. Blend left/right PEC across the centre line: one centre vertex on RIGHT_PEC beside
   one on LEFT_PEC folded a cutout edge into a point when the slider moved each PEC outward. Smooth PEC
   across the under-bust bridge (vertices off the skin by more than max(6 mm, 0.6 × ring spacing)), or it
   forms a stepped shelf.
8. **Calves through the hobble skirt on every step, or the skirt "splits" between the legs.** Unblended
   weights split the tube like trousers; blended weights lag the calves. **Fix:** blend weights in a
   central band and bulge that band outward by as much as the blend lags; keep the steps small (±2° at the
   hip). The quantity that matters is the leg separation at the hem.
9. **Clipping meter stuck at "102 vertices, 14 mm" whatever changed.** It was the hands: they hang beside the
   thighs and counted as skin outside the dress. **Fix:** exclude arm and hand vertices from leg checks.
10. **Small ankle-pivot sway: feet slide, then the body "juts".** The feet stay fixed only if the pelvis
    translation and the pelvis/leg/ankle rotations cancel exactly. After `.bvh` import they didn't (both
    sign variants failed), most likely because import compresses small position and rotation changes
    differently. **Fix:** write `.anim` directly, with every frame as a key. The feet stayed planted, and the
    user found the `.anim` walk "a lot smoother" than the `.bvh` one. Pelvis position keys in `.anim` are
    offsets from rest (confirmed in world). A rotation-only `.bvh` fallback can't slide, but the sway was
    barely visible and it stuttered at the loop.
11. **Zipper slider invisible at the collar.** Parts sized in texture pixels: around the neck one u-pixel is
    ~0.2 mm of fabric, at the hips ~0.6 mm. **Fix:** size texture details in millimetres using a per-row
    metres-per-u measured from the mesh; draw the slider before the teeth and remove the teeth under it.
12. **Leather looked silver in previews.** It was preview lighting: a bright grey world, strong area lights
    and Filmic. **Fix:** Standard view transform with a dim world. Separately, a fully matte black zipper
    tape reads grey under light; roughness ~0.45 reads black.
13. **Doubled side seam.** The u=0/1 wrap is one seam; listing both 0.0 and 1.0 drew it twice. Edge
    stitching also skipped faces whose u is just past 1. **Fix:** list the seam once; stamp boundary
    segments at u−1, u and u+1.

## Assets
No generated art or AI-made assets. All mesh is procedural from the kit body's shape (no kit geometry
shipped). Textures are procedural numpy (pebbled grain, seams, topstitching, interlocking zipper teeth,
slider and pull tab) at 2048². Animations are hand-specified joint curves (sines) in SL's rest frame. The
box image is two transparent EEVEE renders composited with Blender text objects.

## Cost and time
One long session of about 25 in-world iterations (fit, armholes, cutouts, animations). Upload costs only for
the final main-grid release; all testing used Local Mesh and Aditi.
